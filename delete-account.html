Delete your BaeBae account

From the app (fastest)

Open BaeBae → Settings → Account → Delete account → "Delete my account forever". Your profile, messages you sent, photos, voice notes, stories, contacts and credits are deleted right away.

Without the app

Write to support@gruposineva.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Messages you already sent may remain on the other person's phone, without your name. Safety reports are kept only as long as needed to review them.

Borrar tu cuenta (español)

En la app: Ajustes → Cuenta → Eliminar cuenta → «Eliminar mi cuenta para siempre». Sin la app: escribe a support@gruposineva.com desde el correo de tu cuenta con el asunto «Borrar mi cuenta»; la borramos en menos de 30 días y te lo confirmamos.
