BaeBae support

BaeBae translates calls and chats so two people can understand each other in their own languages.

Contact

Write to support@gruposineva.com, or in the app go to Settings → Help → Report a problem. A person on our team reads every message, and safety reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Credits

Live voice translation uses credits: 1 credit = 1 minute in a call, 2 per minute in “Talk in person”, 1 per AI image. Typed chat, voice notes and same-language calls are free. You get 30 credits when you sign up and, every month, a free top-up to 10. If credits run out during a call, the call continues without translation.

Report or block someone

Touch and hold a message, story or AI image and choose “Report”. To block a person, open their contact card and choose “Block”. Groups can be reported from the group’s info screen.

Your data

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use. To delete your account, see Delete your account.
