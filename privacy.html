BaeBae is a messaging and video-calling app that translates so two people can understand each other even without a shared language. This explains what data we use, why and with whom, in plain words.

Who we are

BaeBae is a service of Grupo Sineva Inc. For any question about your data, write to support@gruposineva.com or use "Report a problem" in Settings → Help.

What data we use

Your account: email and password (the password is stored encrypted; we never see it).

Your profile: name, username, photo, phone number if you add it, your language and the voice you choose for translation.

What you share: messages, photos, videos, voice notes, documents, stickers and GIFs, polls, events, reminders, stories (they last 24 hours) and your location only when you choose to send it.

Your calls: who you talk to, when and for how long. Audio and video travel directly between phones, encrypted; when the network does not allow that, they pass encrypted through a relay server (Cloudflare) that cannot see or hear them.

Translations of your messages and the translated phrases of your calls, which stay in the chat as text.

Your translation credits and how you use them (minutes and dates).

Technical data: the identifier used to send you notifications and error reports without the content of your messages.

AI translation (Google)

To translate, BaeBae sends to Google (Gemini models on Google Cloud Vertex AI): your voice during calls and in "Talk in person" while the translator is on; the text of messages and what voice notes say; and what you type to create an AI image.

BaeBae Assistant, the AI assistant, also uses Gemini: it receives what you write in its chat and, in other chats, only the messages that mention it with @BaeBae or reply to it, along with its previous answers; the rest of those conversations is not sent to it. Its answer stays in that chat and everyone in it can see it. When it needs to search the internet, the question goes through Google Search.

We only do this after you give permission in the app, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy (without it the app cannot translate).

Google processes this data on our behalf to return the translation and does not use it to train its models.

Machine translation can be wrong. Do not use it for medical, legal or emergency decisions.

Why we use it

To provide the service: deliver your messages and calls, translate them, notify you, keep your history and your credits.

To keep the app safe: review reports, stop abuse and fix errors.

We do not sell your data, there are no ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

The people you talk to: what you send them.

Providers that help us run the service, only for that: Supabase (database, accounts and files), Google Cloud (AI translation, voice and images; Android notifications through Firebase), Apple (iPhone notifications), Cloudflare (call relay), Expo (app updates), GIPHY (your GIF search terms) and OpenStreetMap (maps).

Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your messages and files, until you delete them or your account. Stories are deleted after 24 hours.

If you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) we delete your profile and your data. Messages you already sent may remain on the recipient’s phone, without your name.

Safety reports are kept as long as needed to review them.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, withdraw the AI permission, pause or delete your account from the app, and ask us for a copy of your data or to correct or delete something by writing to support@gruposineva.com.

If you live in the European Union or anywhere with data protection laws, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Minimum age

BaeBae is for people aged 16 or older. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we close it.

Security

Everything travels encrypted (TLS). Each person can only read their own conversations (access rules in the database) and files are stored encrypted.

Changes

If we change this policy we will tell you in the app before the change applies. Last updated: 2026-10-15.
